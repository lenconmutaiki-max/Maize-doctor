# 🌽 Maize Doctor by Lencon Mutaiki

**Helping Zimbabwe farmers diagnose maize diseases in English & Shona**

Made with Python ❤️ in Zimbabwe

---

## What does it do?

Maize Doctor asks the farmer about the symptoms on their maize leaves, stalk and cob - then tells them:

- ✅ What disease it is
- ✅ How to treat it
- ✅ How to prevent it next season
- ✅ In English AND Shona

## Diseases it can find

- Grey Leaf Spot
- Maize Streak Virus
- Northern Corn Leaf Blight
- Common Rust
- Fall Armyworm damage
- And more!

## How to use it

1. Tap **Open in Colab** above
2. Press **Run All**
3. Answer the questions about your maize
4. Get your diagnosis!
